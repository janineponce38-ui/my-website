About me

I grew up in a farming family where every peso mattered. I paused college to help support my younger siblings’ education, returned to finish my Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, and later built a career across auditing, quality control, and manufacturing.

Since 2019 I have worked in Taiwan as an overseas Filipino worker. Alongside my job, I study AI automation through online learning and training programs, and I plan to enroll in a school in Taiwan next year to deepen those skills.

My story is not about having everything from the beginning. It is about starting with almost nothing and choosing to move forward.

My journey

A short timeline of the steps that shaped me. Each one built the discipline I bring to my work today.

  1. College

    Studied, then paused to support my family

    Finished an Associate in Computer so I could start working and help my two younger siblings stay in school.

  2. First jobs

    Cebu Mitsumi, Dewfoam, Monde Nissin

    Worked in electronics manufacturing, then as an Internal Auditor, then as a Quality Controller. Returned to school and completed my BS in Information Technology.

  3. November 2019

    Became an OFW in Taiwan

    Faced difficult working conditions, reported them to the labor authorities, and started over with a new employer.

  5. Today

  6. Next year

    To apply and grow what I have learned in AI automation.
